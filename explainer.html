PCOM Performance Bonus · Team Guide

How your bonus is calculated.

A plain-English guide to the system. Read it once, refer back as needed. No jargon. No surprises.

Audience: Media Buyers & Team Leader
Scope: Monthly bonus mechanics
You earn a share of a shared team bonus pool based on how well your assigned projects beat their monthly targets. The more you beat them by, the bigger your share. Your slice depends on your performance — not on what others do.
01

The four rules

Everything else flows from these. If you remember nothing else, remember these four.

01
Each project has a baseline
A monthly target based on a weighted average of the past 3 months. The most recent month carries the most weight; older months count less.
02
Beat baseline to earn
Below 100% → that project contributes $0 to your bonus this month. At 100% or above → you earn a share.
03
Beat by more, earn more
Your share grows with your achievement %. Hitting 130% earns more than 105%. There's no ceiling.
04
Two independent pools
Internal projects share one pool. Whizzco has its own. Earning from one doesn't depend on the other.
02

How baseline is calculated

The baseline is your monthly target. It reflects recent performance, not ancient history. This is the formula:

3 months ago× 15%
2 months ago× 20%
Last month× 65%
Sum = Your baseline

Why this works: if you've been trending up, your baseline rises with you — rewards stay realistic. If you've been struggling lately, the baseline drops to give you a fair shot.

Worked example: if a project earned $5K, $7K, and $10K in the last three months (oldest to newest):

$5,000 × 15%$750
$7,000 × 20%$1,400
$10,000 × 65%$6,500
Baseline this month $8,650

You need to beat $8,650 by end of month to earn from this project.

03

The 100% threshold

For each project you're assigned to, your Achievement % is the key number:

Achievement = Projected EOM ÷ Baseline × 100

Projected EOM = your current MTD × (days in the month ÷ days passed). It's an estimate of where you'll land at end-of-month if you keep your current daily pace.

100% · BASELINE
Below = $0
Above = Earning
Below 100% That project contributes nothing to your bonus this month. Other eligible projects still count — you're not locked out everywhere just because one project missed.
100% or above You're eligible from this project. The further you push past 100%, the bigger your share becomes.
04

Two independent pools

The bonus isn't one big pile — it's split into two pools that work independently. Each pool is sized based on profit from its projects only.

INTERNAL
Internal Pool
  • Flareon
  • Brightdrop
  • Crowdx
  • Axen
  • Keylor
Sized based on the combined projected profit of these 5 projects. MBs assigned here compete for shares of this pool by how well they beat their baselines.
WHIZZCO
Whizzco Pool
  • Whizzco
A completely separate pool, sized only on Whizzco's profit. Only MBs assigned to Whizzco can earn from it. Failing your Internal project doesn't disqualify you here.

Why two pools? Whizzco operates differently from the rest of the portfolio — different scale, different network, different mechanics. Mixing it into one pool would distort fairness. Keeping it independent means an MB assigned to both still has two chances to earn, and a struggling pool doesn't drag the other down.

06

Timing & publishing

Your dashboard isn't live data flying around every minute — it's a stable snapshot you can plan against.

Throughout the month your bonus shows as a projection — what you'd earn if you kept your current pace through end-of-month.

At end of month the bonus settles on actual profit, not the projection. The projection is a guidance signal, the actual is the payout.

Your view updates when your manager publishes — not continuously. This avoids numbers wobbling all day as raw data trickles in. When you see a "last updated" timestamp on your dashboard, that's the most recent snapshot.

07

Frequently asked questions

If one project misses baseline, do I lose everything?
No. Each project is measured independently. One miss doesn't disqualify your other projects. You still earn shares from every project that beats its own baseline.
Can I see other team members' bonuses or achievements?
No. Your dashboard shows only your own performance. Comparing isn't part of the system — your share is based on your achievement, not on beating others.
My dashboard showed $X yesterday and $Y today. Why did it change?
Two things can move the projection: (1) your actual performance improved or declined, which updates the projected EOM; (2) your manager published a new snapshot with refreshed data. Final payout uses actual end-of-month profit, not in-month projections.
What if I'm assigned to a brand new project with no history?
Baselines need history to calculate. For new projects, your manager will set an interim target — usually based on early performance patterns. Once enough months pass, the standard weighted formula takes over.
Does going from 100% to 110% double my bonus from that project?
No, it grows incrementally. Going from 100% to 110% increases your share by 10% relative to the 100% baseline contribution. Going from 100% to 200% effectively doubles your contribution. The relationship is proportional.
What counts as "actual" profit at end of month?
Net profit from Voluum tracking, settled at month-end after all postbacks are received. The dashboard's "MTD" number is what's been tracked so far; "EOM actual" is the same number once the month closes and all conversions are confirmed.
Can I pace myself to just barely hit 100% and stop pushing?
Technically you'll be eligible at 100% — but your share will be small. Pushing to 120%, 150%, or higher meaningfully grows your share. The system specifically rewards effort beyond the threshold.
08

Glossary

BASELINE
The monthly target for a project. Calculated as a weighted average of the last 3 months' actual profit (15% / 20% / 65%).
MTD
Month to Date. The actual profit recorded so far in the current month.
EOM
End of Month. The closing point for the bonus period.
PROJECTED EOM
An estimate of where the project's profit will land at month-end if the current daily pace continues. Formula: MTD × (days in month ÷ days passed).
ACHIEVEMENT %
How much the projected EOM exceeds the baseline. Projected EOM ÷ Baseline × 100. Above 100% = eligible.
INTERNAL POOL
The bonus pool sized on combined profit from Flareon, Brightdrop, Crowdx, Axen, and Keylor.
WHIZZCO POOL
A separate, independent bonus pool sized only on Whizzco's profit.
ELIGIBLE
A project where your projected EOM beats or equals the baseline. Eligible projects contribute to your bonus; non-eligible ones contribute $0.
PUBLISHED
When the manager presses "Publish to Team", a snapshot of the data becomes visible to MBs and TL. Your view updates only at that moment.
